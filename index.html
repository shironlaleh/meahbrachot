A quiet companion for the mitzva of one hundred daily brachot. Log the tefillot and meals of your day in a tap, count each bracha you say, and watch the ring fill toward one hundred — with the day resetting at nightfall for wherever you are.

  • Counts what you actually said — nothing assumed
  • Halachic day boundaries and zmanim computed for your location
  • Full blessing texts with word-by-word English
  • Reminders anchored to the day's real zmanim, each on its own switch
  • Optional streaks — consecutive days of the hundred, Shabbat-aware
  • Edut HaMizraḥ, Sefard, and Ashkenaz; a women's count; English and Hebrew
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking — your count stays on your phone
