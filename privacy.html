Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

The short version: Meah Brachot has no accounts and no sign-in. Your daily count, your settings, and your location stay on your phone. The only things that ever leave the device are anonymous usage statistics that can't identify you, and any feedback you choose to send us.

What stays on your device

  • Your brachot log — every bracha you count, every day. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your settings — language, nusach, whether you count as a man or a woman, your reminder choices, clock format, and the optional first name you may enter. The first name is used only for the app's own wording; it is never sent to us.
  • Your location — see below.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Location

Zmanim (the halachic times that decide when your day resets and when reminders fire) depend on where you are. With your permission, the app checks your location once — when you set up the app, when you tap "Use my location," or after a change of time zone if you leave automatic travel updates on. The app keeps the coordinates and city name on your device to compute zmanim. It does not track you in the background, and your location is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

If you prefer not to share location, you can choose a city from a list instead.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No notification server is involved, and nothing about your count or your reminders leaves the device.

Blessing texts from Sefaria

The app can display the full text of each bracha. Those texts are loaded from the public Sefaria library over the internet and cached on your phone. Like any web request, this lets Sefaria's servers see standard connection information such as your IP address. No personal data, and nothing about your count, is included in these requests. Sefaria's own privacy policy governs their servers.

Anonymous usage statistics

So we can tell whether anyone is using the app, it records a few anonymous events in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database:

  • a random identifier generated on your phone when you first open the app (it is not your Apple ID, your name, or any device serial number, and it cannot be linked back to you);
  • the type of event — the app was opened on a given day, setup was completed, or the daily goal was reached;
  • the date, your chosen language and nusach, and the app version.

That is the complete list. Your count, your log, your name, and your location are never part of it. We use these statistics only to understand how many people use the app. They are not used for advertising, are not sold, and are not shared with any third party.

Feedback you send

If you use the in-app feedback form, we receive the message you write, the contact detail you optionally provide, your app language, and basic context about the app (such as its version) to help us understand your report. We use it only to respond to you and improve the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, passwords, or sign-in of any kind.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children

Meah Brachot is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and deletion

  • Delete the app to remove your log, settings, and location from your device.
  • Deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings at any time; the app will still work with a chosen city.
  • Turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • To have your feedback messages or anonymous usage records deleted, email us (below). Because the usage records are anonymous, please include the approximate dates you used the app so we can locate them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
